Supports we provide

The right support, in the right setting.

Needs change, so support should too. These are the supports we build around each young person and family.

Crisis Respite

Short-term, around-the-clock care in a safe home when a young person's current living situation has broken down, with a plan to get them back to stability.

Respite Care

Planned breaks for families and caregivers, in the home or out of it, so everyone can reset while the young person is well cared for.

Community Residential Services

A staffed, licensed home with 24-hour support for youth who need more structure than a family setting can offer right now.

Individualized Home Supports

In-home coaching and skill building for the young person, with training for the family so progress holds after we leave.

Positive Support Services

Behavior assessment and a practical support plan from qualified professionals, focused on what the behavior is telling us.

Transition Planning

Hands-on coordination with families, counties, schools, and care teams so the move home or to a next placement sticks.

Paying for services: Many supports can be funded through Minnesota disability waivers. Ask your county case manager, or contact us and we will help you find out what applies.

About us

Built by someone who has done the work.

Solara Services was founded by Darris Garner, a behavior specialist with more than seven years in behavioral health. He has spent those years on the floor with people in their hardest moments, writing support plans, coaching staff through crisis, and sitting with families who had run out of options.

As a Designated Coordinator in Minnesota's 245D system, Darris has also carried the responsibility behind the scenes: making sure each person's plan is followed, staff are trained, and the documentation holds up. He has seen what good care looks like from both sides, and what happens when it is missing.

He started Solara because too many kids in crisis end up waiting in places that were never meant for them. Solara is meant to be the place that says yes, and then does the work well.

Our approach

Calm, consistent, and centered on the young person.

01

Stabilize first

Before anything else, a young person needs to feel safe. We lead with structure, predictability, and staff who do not rattle.

02

Know the person

Every plan starts with who this young person is, what they are good at, and what sets them off. Behavior is communication, and we listen.

03

Plan the next step

Crisis care is a bridge. We work with families, counties, and care teams from day one on where this young person goes next.

Referrals

Making a referral should not be the hard part.

Case managers and families can reach a real person, get a straight answer on fit and openings, and know what happens next.

Start a Referral
  1. 1

    Reach out

    Call or send the referral form. Tell us what is going on and how soon support is needed.

  2. 2

    We review fit

    Our team looks at needs, safety, and current openings, and gets back to you as quickly as we can.

  3. 3

    Plan the start

    We coordinate intake with the family and care team so day one feels steady, not rushed.

Our promises

What you can count on from us.

To young people

You are safe here, and you are heard.

We will learn who you are before we talk about what went wrong. You get a say in your own plan, and adults who stay steady on the hard days.

To families

You will never be left guessing.

We tell you what is happening, in plain language, and we ask what works at home. You know your child best, and we treat you that way.

To county partners

Straight answers and clean paperwork.

We answer the phone, tell you honestly whether we are the right fit, and keep documentation you can rely on.
